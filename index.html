Clipper

Clipper is a small self-hosted tool, operated by one individual, that publishes short vertical excerpts (YouTube Shorts and TikTok drafts) of the French YouTube channels Vosges FM and ne t'abonne pas, with the explicit agreement of their creator.

How it works

  1. New episodes are transcribed and the tool proposes 2–3 short clips (30–75 s).
  2. A human reviews every clip, its title and description, and approves it manually.
  3. Only approved clips are uploaded, about twice a day, to one dedicated clips channel that we own. Each description credits the original creator and links to the full episode.

Clipper uses YouTube API Services (upload only). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
