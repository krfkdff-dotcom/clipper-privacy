Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Clipper is a self-hosted tool operated by one individual. It publishes short excerpts of the YouTube channels "Vosges FM" and "ne t'abonne pas" (with their creator's agreement) to dedicated clips accounts on YouTube and TikTok that we own.

YouTube API Services

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using Clipper you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Please also see the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

Only the OAuth authorization of our own YouTube channel and our own TikTok account, limited to uploading videos (YouTube scope youtube.upload; TikTok scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). We do not access, collect or store any data about other users, viewers or channels.

What we store and why

The OAuth tokens of our own accounts, stored on our own server in files readable only by the tool, and the list of videos we uploaded (video ID and date), to avoid duplicate uploads. This data is never shared with or sold to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted as soon as access is revoked or no longer needed. You can ask for the deletion of any data at the address below; we will do it within 30 days.

Revoking access

Google account: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions
TikTok: in the TikTok app, Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
